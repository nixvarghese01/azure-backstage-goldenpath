# Getting help

This is a **model project**: a reference design for an Internal Developer Portal on Azure, maintained by
one person in their own time. There is no hosted service, SLA or paid support.

## Before asking

Most answers are already written down:

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| What the IDP does and how the golden path works | [README](README.md), [golden path](docs/developer-guide/golden-path.md) |
| Running it locally or on a free Azure subscription | [README: local development](README.md#local-development), [demo environment](docs/operations/demo-environment.md) |
| Something failed (a workflow, a request, the portal) | [Troubleshooting](docs/operations/troubleshooting.md) |
| Why it deviates from enterprise best practice | [Best practice vs. free tier](docs/best-practice-vs-free-tier.md) |
| Versions and upgrades | [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md), [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) |
| Design decisions | [Architecture decision records](docs/adr/) |

## Asking a question or reporting a problem

- **A bug** (it doesn't behave as documented): open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/nixvarghese01/azure-backstage-goldenpath/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
  Include the versions, what you ran and the error or the failing workflow run.
- **An idea or a missing feature:** open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/nixvarghese01/azure-backstage-goldenpath/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
  Check the [roadmap](https://github.com/users/nixvarghese01/projects/4) first.
- **A question** that isn't a bug or a feature: open a
  [blank issue](https://github.com/nixvarghese01/azure-backstage-goldenpath/issues/new) and say which
  document you read.
- **A security vulnerability:** never in a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Issues are answered on a best-effort basis. Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
